About gameStoB

Building the future of gaming retail since 2015.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, gameStoB began as a single neon-lit store with a passion for bringing gamers together. What started as a local hub for physical titles and collectibles has grown into one of Indonesia's most trusted gaming marketplaces.

We blend in-store experiences with a seamless digital catalog so every player — casual or competitive — can discover, buy, and play without friction. Our team lives and breathes games, and that shows in every interaction.

Mission

To make premium gaming accessible to everyone through fair pricing, expert curation, and outstanding customer support across digital and physical channels.

Vision

To become Southeast Asia's leading gaming lifestyle brand — where community, commerce, and culture converge under one trusted name: gameStoB.

Values

Integrity in every sale, inclusivity for all players, innovation in how we shop, and relentless dedication to the games we love.
